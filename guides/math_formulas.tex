\textbf{Shape / Solid} & \textbf{Area / Volume Formula} \\ \hline

Sphere & $V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3, \quad SA = 4 \pi r^2$ \\ \hline
Cylinder & $V = \pi r^2 h, \quad SA = 2\pi r h + 2\pi r^2$ \\ \hline
Cone & $V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h, \quad SA = \pi r s + \pi r^2 \quad (s = \text{slant height})$ \\ \hline
Right Prism & $V = B h, \quad SA = 2B + p h \quad (B = \text{base area})$ \\ \hline
Pyramid & $V = \frac{1}{3} B h$ \\ \hline
